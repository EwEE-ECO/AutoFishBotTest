Setup

Installation & Launch

Follow these steps to start using the bot in minutes.

1

Download the bot

Click the "Download" button or join our Telegram channel.

2

Disable antivirus

Antivirus may block the bot (false positive due to input emulation).

3

Configure the game

Copy the graphics settings, ESC → Settings → Graphics.

4

Run as administrator

Right-click AutoFishBot.exe → "Run as administrator".

5

Open the menu

Press Ctrl + Shift + 1 the control interface appears.

6

Choose a mode

Fishing, oranges, lumberjack, or quarry your choice.

7

Done!

Enjoy automation. The bot works stably and efficiently!
